# LinkedIn for Blue-Collar Jobs in India: Product Design Case Study

A product design case study exploring how LinkedIn could help skilled blue-collar workers in India find and secure trustworthy local jobs.

**Live interactive wireframes:** https://pm-sanjana.github.io/linkedin-blue-collar-case-study/

> **Honesty note.** This is a hypothesis-based concept. There are no user interviews, research statistics, or validated outcomes in this project. The persona is a proto-persona built from assumptions. All jobs, wages, and employers shown are illustrative sample data. See the [validation plan](docs/03-validation-plan.md) for how each hypothesis would be tested.

## The challenge

> Design LinkedIn for blue-collar jobs in India.

**Goal:** Help blue-collar workers discover and secure suitable, trustworthy jobs in their locality.
**Primary segment:** Workers without formal educational qualifications seeking hourly, contractual, or full-time work.
**Platform:** Mobile app. **Geography:** India.

## The idea in one paragraph

A résumé can't carry a tradesperson's credibility, because their work is their credential. The concept is a **Skill Passport**: a profile built from the worker's trade, experience, optional photo and video proof, and vouches from past employers. It sits on top of a simple, regional-language, voice-optional local job feed with clear wages and a visible application status. The bet is that **skills proof and career growth**, not just job listings, is what a LinkedIn product could uniquely offer.

## Five screens in the prototype

1. Language selection and onboarding
2. Worker profile creation (skills plus optional proof of work)
3. Home with recommended local jobs
4. Job details and Apply
5. Application status tracking, plus a "Did you start?" confirmation and vouch request

## Design principles

Regional-language support · Voice input as an option, not a requirement · Simple navigation and large tap targets · Relevant jobs by skill, location, wage, and employment type · Clear employer and job information · Optional proof-of-work photos and videos · Low-bandwidth awareness · Privacy and safety

## Proposed North Star

**Verified job starts per week**, meaning both the worker and the employer confirm the start.
Supporting metrics: profile completion, relevant job discovery, application conversion, employer response rate, and job legitimacy. Details are in the [validation plan](docs/03-validation-plan.md).
